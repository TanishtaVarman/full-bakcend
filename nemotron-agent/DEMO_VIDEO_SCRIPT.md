# CrimeBook: 3-Minute Demo Video Script & Storyboard
> **Duration:** 2 minutes 40 seconds (Devpost rule: strictly under 3 minutes)  
> **Platform:** YouTube (Public or Unlisted URL)

---

### [0:00 - 0:25] Scene 1: The Problem — Vanishing Study Chats
**Screen Visual:**
- Title card or webcam: *"CrimeBook: Personal AI Mistake Notebook & Study Companion"*.
- Show an ordinary chat session with endless questions lost in history.

**Voiceover / Spoken Script:**
> *"Hi everyone! When we study using AI chatbots, whether it's for coding interviews, math exams, or history tests, we run into a huge problem: we ask questions, struggle with concepts, and then our mistakes vanish into chat history.
> We keep tripping on the same topics — like C++ function pointers or 3D geometry formulas — without any dedicated place to track our weaknesses.
> This is CrimeBook: an always-on, private personal AI assistant built for the Nebius x NVIDIA Hackathon's Personal AI Track that turns your study mistakes into your biggest learning advantage."*

---

### [0:25 - 0:55] Scene 2: View 1 — Study Chat & Real-Time Mistake Detection
**Screen Visual:**
- Show the app at `http://localhost:3000`. Highlight the warm light pink, beige, and maroon theme matching the design mockup.
- Select **Maths: Geometry** or **DSA: C++ Functions**.
- Type in the bottom pill input: *"can you expand on the topic in more easy terms? I didn't get C++ function creation with pointers"*.
- Click send. Show the tutor's patient reply, and highlight the toast notification:
  `📕 Logged to Mistake Book: "C++ Function Creation & Pointer Syntax"`.

**Voiceover / Spoken Script:**
> *"Here is the CrimeBook dashboard. In View 1, our Study Chat, you can study any topic or uploaded notes.
> Look at what happens when I ask: 'can you expand on the topic in more easy terms? I didn't get C++ function creation'.
> In the background, NVIDIA Nemotron Nano on Nebius Token Factory runs in under 200 milliseconds to analyze the conversation.
> It notices we are struggling, extracts the core confusion point, and automatically logs it straight into our private Crime Book!"*

---

### [0:55 - 1:35] Scene 3: View 2 — The Mistake Book Notepad & Side Chatbot
**Screen Visual:**
- In the sidebar, click on **"Mistake Book"** under the active subject.
- Show the split-screen view:
  - Left: The lined notepad listing tracked trouble spots ("3D Pythagorean Distance", "Pointer Syntax", frequency counts, rules to remember).
  - Right: The dedicated side chatbot.
- Ask a question in the side chatbot: *"Why does int* ptr need an asterisk?"*
- Show the response and demonstrate how the notepad updates dynamically.

**Voiceover / Spoken Script:**
> *"Now let's open View 2: our Mistake Book.
> Instead of a generic chat, it opens into an elegant, bulleted notepad of everything we tripped up on: the exact trigger questions, how many times we struggled, and golden rules to remember.
> And on the right side, we have an always-on side chatbot. You can ask follow-up questions while looking at your notes, and any new difficulty immediately updates back into your Crime Book in real time."*

---

### [1:35 - 2:05] Scene 4: View 3 — Adaptive Diagnostic Tests
**Screen Visual:**
- Click on **"Test"** in the sidebar (or click "Generate Test").
- Show the generated test questions tailored to the student's logged mistakes.
- Point out the "Search Paper" metadata card in the bottom right corner (Topic, Date, Keywords).
- Fill in or demonstrate the answer boxes, then click **"Submit for Corrections"**.

**Voiceover / Spoken Script:**
> *"Now, how do we know if we've actually mastered our mistakes?
> We go to View 3: Test.
> Nemotron Nano reads our Mistake Book and dynamically generates a targeted diagnostic exam testing our exact weak spots.
> In the corner, we have our Search Paper details card.
> Let's submit our answers to get evaluated."*

---

### [2:05 - 2:35] Scene 5: View 4 — Test Corrections & Nemotron 3 Ultra Diagnostics
**Screen Visual:**
- Show View 4: Test Corrections.
- Scroll through Question 1:
  - Highlight Section 1: **Correct Model Answer** (green box).
  - Highlight Section 2: **Your Answers and Mistakes Made** (red/rose box).
  - Point out the diagnostic tag: *"Concept Misunderstanding"* or *"Formula / Calculation Error"*, what went wrong, and the remedy tip.
- Click **"Sync New Gaps to Crime Book"**.

**Voiceover / Spoken Script:**
> *"And here is View 4: Test Corrections, evaluated by NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Ultra.
> Look at the layout: First, we have the complete, step-by-step correct model answer.
> Directly under it, we see our answer compared against it, diagnosing exactly what went wrong — whether it was a conceptual gap, a formula error, or a syntax slip, along with a concrete remedy rule.
> With one click, any new gaps sync back into our Mistake Book so our next study session stays laser-focused."*

---

### [2:35 - 2:40] Scene 6: Open Infrastructure & Closing
**Screen Visual:**
- Open the Settings dialog showing Nebius Token Factory and Tavily keys.
- Show the open source GitHub repository with MIT License.

**Voiceover / Spoken Script:**
> *"CrimeBook gives students persistent, private memory and turns mistakes into mastery using NVIDIA Nemotron on Nebius open cloud infrastructure.
> Thank you so much!"*
